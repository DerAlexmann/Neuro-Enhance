Changes made by the Silberkorn project (werkzeuge/outpaint_export.py,
https://github.com/DerAlexmann/Silberkorn):

  - The LoRA flux-outpaint-lora.safetensors is merged into the weights of the
    transformer with a scale of 1.1.
  - Transformer, VAE encoder and VAE decoder are converted to ONNX. The
    encoder returns the mean of the latent distribution and takes images in
    the range 0..1; the decoder returns images in the range 0..1.
  - The weights of the matrix multiplications of the transformer are
    quantised to 8-bit integers (MatMulNBits, block size 128, symmetric);
    its other weights are stored in 32-bit, those of the VAE in 16-bit
    floating point.
  - The weights of the transformer are split into several files.
  - flux2-klein-outpaint-daten.npz holds the embedding of the fixed prompt
    "Fill the green spaces according to the image", computed with the text
    encoder of FLUX.2 [klein] base 4B (Qwen3, Apache License 2.0), the
    batch-norm statistics of the VAE and the settings of the scheduler.
